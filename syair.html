Keep Moving On

Written by: Stefanie Odelia Akay

Lyrics

When the morning starts to shine,
I leave yesterday behind.
Every step may feel so slow,
But there's still a way to go.

When the road is hard to see,
I believe in what can be.
Even when the rain comes down,
I will keep my feet on the ground.

Keep moving on, don't turn away,
There will be light after the gray.
Take a breath and carry on,
Every night will meet the dawn.

Dreams may change along the way,
But I'll keep trying every day.
I may fall, I may be wrong,
But I'll keep moving on.

Makna Lagu

"Keep Moving On" menceritakan tentang semangat untuk terus melangkah meskipun menghadapi kesulitan. Lagu ini menggambarkan seseorang yang belajar dari masa lalu dan tetap percaya pada masa depan.
